## Short description
Flick a spring-legged robot downhill, spin for power, land on its feet. Heads fly!

## Description (EN)
One flick sends a spring-legged robot flying down the hill. Spin it in mid-air to build up energy, then land on its
feet: charged springs bounce it even further. Missed a landing? Arms and legs pop off on the way, and when the last one
is gone the head fires its rocket and keeps going on its own.

- 5 robots with their own physics, sound, MK2 upgrade and colour schemes
- Campaign: 100 rounds against rival robots and bosses, three star challenges per round
- 9 worlds: Green Hills, the Moon, Supergravity, Chocolate, Jelly, Portals, Chasms, the Cavern and the Exclusion Zone
- Free Run, 38 achievements, three player profiles, 13 languages
- Hand-painted 1930s-cartoon look
- No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no internet needed

Epic Flick is an independent, non-commercial fan project inspired by classic physics arcade games. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the makers of any other game. All characters, art, code and sounds were
made for this project.

## Описание (RU)
Один щелчок — и пружинный робот летит вниз по склону. Крути его в воздухе, копи энергию вращения и приземляйся

5 роботов, кампания из 100 раундов, 9 миров, свободный забег, 38 достижений, 3 профиля, 13 языков.
Рисованная графика в духе мультфильмов 1930-х. Без рекламы, без покупок, без аккаунтов, без интернета.

Независимый некоммерческий проект, вдохновлённый классическими физическими аркадами. Не связан с авторами других игр.

## Installation (Android)
1. Download `EpicShalban_v36.10.apk`.
2. Open it on the phone; allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser / file manager when Android asks.
3. If the Google Play test version is installed, uninstall it first (different signature; Android refuses to update
   it). Note: uninstalling removes that version's local progress.
4. SHA-256 of the file: see the changelog below / the file listing.
